Every participant on Stellar has an account, identified by a public key that starts with `G`.

## Looking up an account

Paste the public key into the search bar, or click any account address you see in the explorer. If the account owns a Soroban domain, you can search for the domain instead.

## Account details

- **Balances:** XLM and every asset the account holds. Liquidity pool shares link to the pool page.
- **Trustlines:** the assets the account is allowed to hold, with limits.
- **Signers:** keys authorized to sign for the account, with their weights.
- **Thresholds:** the signing weight required for low, medium, and high security operations.
- **Data entries:** key-value data stored on the account.
- **Domains:** a badge such as `alice.xlm` when the account owns a domain. See [Soroban Domains](/explorer/domains).

## Activity

Three tabs show the account's history:

- **Transactions** that involve the account.
- **Operations** that affected it.
- **Effects**, the individual state changes, such as a credit or a trustline update.

Lists are paginated. Scroll to load more.
