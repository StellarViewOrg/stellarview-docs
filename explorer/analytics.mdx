title: "Network analytics"
description: "Network-wide Stellar activity in StellarView Explorer: transaction counts, volume, fees, accounts, asset supply, and Top-N rankings."
---

The **Analytics** section charts activity across the whole network. The data comes from the indexer's [analytics API](/api-reference/introduction).

## History

Time series at hourly, daily, or weekly resolution for:

- Transaction count
- Transaction volume (native XLM transfers)
- Classic and Soroban fees
- Active accounts and new accounts
- Asset supply, summed across all assets or filtered to one asset by code and issuer, or by Soroban contract ID

Export any chart as CSV or JSON.

## Top N

Ranked tables over a selectable time window: the most active contracts, the most active assets, and the highest fees.

## When a metric is missing

Each chart queries the indexer on its own. If one metric hasn't been aggregated, only that chart shows "Not available yet", and the others keep loading.
