A ledger is the state of the network after a set of transactions is applied. A new one closes about every 5 seconds.

## Browsing ledgers

The **Ledgers** page lists recent ledgers, newest first. For each one you see:

- **Sequence number**, its position in the chain.
- **Closed at**, when it was finalized.
- **Transaction count** and **operation count**.

## Live updates

New ledgers stream in as they close and appear at the top of the list. You don't need to refresh.

## Ledger details

Open a ledger to see its header and every transaction it includes.
