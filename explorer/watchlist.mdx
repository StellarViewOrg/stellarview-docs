---
title: "Watchlist"
description: "Save Stellar accounts and assets to a local watchlist in StellarView Explorer."
---

The watchlist keeps the accounts and assets you care about one click away.

## Adding and removing items

On any account or asset page, click the bookmark icon. Click it again to remove the item.

## Viewing the watchlist

Open **Watchlist** from the navigation. Items are grouped by type:

- **Accounts**, with their current XLM balance.
- **Assets**, with code and issuer.

## Where it's stored

The watchlist lives in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing syncs between devices.

<Tip>
  Clearing your browser data or switching browsers starts you with an empty watchlist.
</Tip>
