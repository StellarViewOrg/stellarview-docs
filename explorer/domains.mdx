title: "Soroban Domains"
description: "Resolve, browse, and inspect *.xlm domains in StellarView Explorer."
---

Soroban Domains is a name service on Stellar. It maps names like `stellar.xlm` or `pay.alice.xlm` to an account or a contract.

## Resolving a domain

Type the name into the search bar or the command palette. The explorer resolves it against the registry contract and opens the account or contract it points to.

Resolution runs directly against Stellar RPC, so it works even before the indexer has caught up. A name that isn't registered shows "not registered" instead of a raw contract error.

<Note>
  Resolution is not available on Futurenet, where the registry contract is not deployed.
</Note>

## Badges on account and contract pages

An address that owns a domain shows it as a badge next to the address, such as `alice.xlm`, plus `+N more` when it owns several. No badge means it owns none. A muted **Domains pending** badge means the indexer hasn't caught up yet, which is a different situation.

## The domains browser

The **Domains** section lists registrations with a status filter (active, expired, revoked, or all) and pagination. Each domain page shows:

- **Status:** active, expired, or revoked.
- **Resolved target**, linked to its account or contract.
- **Owner.**
- **Registration and expiry dates.**
- **Event history:** register, transfer, renew, claim, and revoke, each linked to its transaction.

<Note>
  Search in the domains list matches exact names only for now. The read API doesn't support prefix search yet.
</Note>
